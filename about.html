ABOUT US

Sri Lanka's Leading Mobile Phone Repairing Training Center

Since 2006, ATHEEQ Technical Institute has been empowering students with the practical knowledge and technical skills needed to succeed in the mobile phone repair industry.

OUR STORY

Two Decades of Technical Excellence

ATHEEQ Technical Institute was founded in 2006 with a single mission: to provide world-class technical education that is accessible to all. Over the past 20 years, we have trained more than 8,000 students who are now working as professional mobile phone repair technicians across Sri Lanka and beyond.

Our institute is recognized as the largest and most trusted mobile phone repairing training center in Sri Lanka, offering structured courses, modern equipment, and experienced instructors.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, practical technical education in mobile phone repairing that enables students to build successful careers and contribute to the Sri Lankan economy.

Our Vision

To be the most recognized and respected technical training institute in Sri Lanka, producing skilled professionals who lead the mobile phone repair industry.
